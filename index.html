PerfectPitch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

PerfectPitch ("the app") is an ear-training game for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing automatically; an error report reaches us only if you decide to send it by email.

Data we collect

Nothing automatically. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Your progress

Your XP, streak, stars, statistics and trophies are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app erases them; Settings › Progress erases them too.

In-app purchase (PerfectPitch Pro)

The app is free. PerfectPitch Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy: we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the Pro version was bought on your Google account, and remembers the answer on your device. We do not receive your name or email address from the purchase, except what Google Play shows in its developer reports for order management and refunds.

Streak reminder (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the app shows one reminder in the evening, at the time chosen in Settings › Streak reminder, and only on days you have not played yet. The reminder is scheduled and shown on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in the app or in Android's settings.

Error reports (optional)

To help fix problems, the app keeps a technical log on your device: what it did (pages opened, settings changed, rounds played with their score, sound start-up), app errors, and, on Android 11 and later, the reason Android gives when the app stopped (crash, not responding, low memory). The log is size-limited, stays in the app's private storage and is erased when you uninstall the app or press Clear in Settings › Error report.

The log is sent only if you choose to: Send by email opens your own email app with the report filled in, addressed to the developer. You can read or edit it, and nothing is sent until you press Send. A report contains the app version, your phone model and Android version, the app settings (language, theme, note names, instrument, game sounds), error messages and the recent log. It contains no audio, no contacts and no location. Like any email, it shows your email address to the developer. Reports are used only to fix the app, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted once the problem is solved. You can ask for a report to be deleted at the address below.

Permissions

The app does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts or your files. It uses: "modify audio settings" (to play sound), "show notifications" (the streak reminder, asked once on Android 13 and later), "Google Play billing" (the Pro purchase), and the permissions Android's scheduling library needs to show the reminder at the right time, even after a restart (keep the phone awake briefly, start after boot, network state). Only the notification permission is asked; Android grants the others without asking.

Network

The app does not need an internet connection to play and does not send any data itself. The daily challenge and the daily quests are computed on your device from the date. The Pro purchase goes through the Google Play app. Error reports go through your email app, only when you send them.

Children

The app collects no data automatically from anyone, including children.
